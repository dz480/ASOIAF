# 1. Narrative Context

After **Robert Baratheon** overthrows **House Targaryen**, the surviving royal siblings **Viserys** and **Daenerys** wander among the **FREE CITIES**, dependent on wealthier patrons. Viserys dreams of restoring his rule over **Westeros**; Daenerys longs instead for the house with the red door in **Braavos**. Their host in **PENTOS**, **Illyrio Mopatis**, arranges for Daenerys to meet **Khal Drogo**, a powerful **Dothraki** warlord. Viserys believes that marrying her to the khal will secure the mounted army he lacks. Daenerys begins without independent wealth, political authority or a safe home, and is expected to submit to her brother's bargain.

# 4. Narrative Overview

**CSRM chapters (publication order):** [Ch. 3](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-3) · [Ch. 11](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-11) · [Ch. 23](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-23) · [Ch. 36](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-36) · [Ch. 46](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-46) · [Ch. 54](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-54) · [Ch. 61](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-61) · [Ch. 64](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-64) · [Ch. 68](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-68) · [Ch. 72](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-72)

In **PENTOS**, **Daenerys Targaryen** lives under her brother **Viserys**'s control while their patron **Illyrio Mopatis** arranges her marriage to **Khal Drogo** in return for promised military support. **Jorah Mormont**, an exiled Westerosi knight, attends their introduction. At the wedding, Daenerys receives a silver mare, 3 dragon eggs and 3 handmaids. Travelling across the **DOTHRAKI SEA**, she learns riding from **Irri**, language from **Jhiqui** and intimate customs from **Doreah**. Her confidence and affection for Drogo grow; she defies Viserys, orders **Jhogo** to restrain him and realises she is pregnant.

In **VAES DOTHRAK**, her brother's humiliation intensifies. Daenerys eats a stallion's heart before the **dosh khaleen**, who foretell that her unborn son **Rhaego** will be the stallion who mounts the world. At the sacred feast, Viserys threatens her with a drawn sword and demands his promised crown. Drogo has him restrained and kills him with molten gold.

Drogo initially refuses to cross the sea. During a visit to the Western Market, Jorah unmasks a wine seller's attempt to poison Daenerys; she has also tried heating a dragon egg without effect. Outraged by the assassination attempt on his pregnant wife, Drogo vows to invade **Westeros** and win the **Iron Throne** for Rhaego.

His khalasar defeats rival riders and raids a **LHAZAREEN** village. Daenerys intervenes to protect captive women, despite objections from Drogo's warriors. The godswife **Mirri Maz Duur**, among those she protects, offers to treat Drogo's wounds. His condition worsens until he falls from his horse, jeopardising both his life and his leadership. Daenerys halts the khalasar and authorises Mirri's blood-magic rite, which requires life in exchange for life. As shadows move within the tent, **Qotho** kills **Quaro** and Jorah kills Qotho; **Rakharo**, assisted by Jhogo, kills **Haggo**. Daenerys enters labour amid the fighting, and Jorah carries her into the tent.

After a prolonged unconsciousness, Daenerys learns that Rhaego was stillborn and Drogo remains alive but wholly unresponsive. Most riders have dispersed, leaving fewer than 100 followers; **Eroeh**, a captive Daenerys had sheltered, has been killed. Mirri recounts the devastation inflicted on her people and the price of the ritual. Daenerys tends Drogo, then ends his life.

For the funeral, she frees the remaining enslaved people, asserts her claim as Viserys's heir and offers the survivors a place beside her. She gives weapons to Jhogo, **Aggo** and Rakharo, seeking their loyalty; Jorah swears as her first Queensguard. She lays the eggs upon Drogo's funeral pyre, orders Mirri bound to it and walks into the flames. At dawn she emerges alive with **3 newly hatched dragons**, and the remaining Dothraki kneel before her.

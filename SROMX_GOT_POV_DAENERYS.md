# 1. Narrative Context

After **Robert Baratheon** overthrows **House Targaryen**, the surviving royal siblings **Viserys** and **Daenerys** wander among the **FREE CITIES**, dependent on wealthier patrons. Viserys dreams of restoring his rule over **Westeros**; Daenerys longs instead for the house with the red door in **Braavos**. Their host in **PENTOS**, **Illyrio Mopatis**, arranges for Daenerys to meet **Khal Drogo**, a powerful **Dothraki** warlord. Viserys believes that marrying her to the khal will secure the mounted army he lacks. Daenerys begins without independent wealth, political authority or a safe home, and is expected to submit to her brother's bargain.

# 4. Narrative Overview

In **PENTOS**, **Daenerys Targaryen** depends upon her brother **Viserys** and their host **Illyrio Mopatis**. Viserys arranges for her to marry **Khal Drogo**, believing the match will obtain an army to retake Westeros. The exiled knight **Jorah Mormont** attends their introduction. At the wedding outside the city, Daenerys receives 3 dragon eggs, a silver mare and 3 handmaids. Drogo's gift gives her a new freedom of movement as the khalasar journeys east.

Crossing the **DOTHRAKI SEA**, Dany struggles with the riding and isolation before growing accustomed to her role. **Irri** teaches horsemanship, **Jhiqui** the Dothraki language, and **Doreah** offers personal guidance. Daenerys develops affection for Drogo and learns to direct the riders around her. When Viserys tries to reassert his authority, **Jhogo** restrains him and Dany refuses to relent. Soon afterwards she recognises that she is pregnant.

At **VAES DOTHRAK**, Viserys grows increasingly bitter at his position, while Daenerys prepares for the rites of the sacred city. She eats the heart of a stallion before the **dosh khaleen**, whose prophetesses proclaim that her unborn son will be the stallion who mounts the world. She names him **Rhaego**. During the subsequent feast Viserys enters with a sword, threatens his sister and demands the promised crown. Drogo has him held and kills him with molten gold.

Dany presses Jorah about returning west, though Drogo sees little reason to cross the sea. At Vaes Dothrak's Western Market, a seller offers Dany rare wine. Jorah demands that the man taste it first; his attempt to escape exposes a plot to poison her. Dany also experiments with heating a dragon egg, without success. Drogo, enraged by the attempted assassination of his pregnant wife, pledges to cross the narrow sea and conquer the **Seven Kingdoms** for Rhaego.

Drogo's riders move into the **Lhazareen** lands and defeat a rival khalasar. Dany sees the devastation of a raided settlement and intervenes to protect captive women, despite resistance from Drogo's warriors. Among the survivors is the godswife **Mirri Maz Duur**, who offers to heal the khal's wounds. Drogo initially dismisses his injuries, but his condition worsens during the march. Eventually he falls from his horse, undermining his position in a society that expects a khal to ride.

Daenerys halts the khalasar and calls Mirri. Jorah warns that Drogo's death would disperse the army, but Dany consents to a dangerous blood-magic rite requiring a life in exchange for life. Mirri sacrifices Drogo's stallion, and mysterious shadows move against the glowing tent. **Qotho** attempts to interrupt the ceremony and kills **Quaro**. Jorah kills Qotho; **Rakharo**, aided by Jhogo, kills **Haggo**. Daenerys begins labour amid the fighting, and Jorah carries her into the ritual tent.

When Dany eventually wakes, she discovers that Rhaego was stillborn and Drogo survives without meaningful awareness. Most of his followers have abandoned the camp, leaving fewer than 100 people. She also learns that **Eroeh**, whom she had protected during the raid, was killed after the khalasar fragmented. Mirri describes the destruction of her people and the cost of the ritual. Dany tends Drogo tenderly before ending his life.

At Drogo's funeral, Daenerys identifies herself as Viserys's heir, frees the remaining slaves and offers the survivors a place in her following. She gives arms to **Jhogo**, **Aggo** and Rakharo, asking for their service, while Jorah pledges himself as her first Queensguard. Dany lays the dragon eggs beside Drogo, orders Mirri bound to the pyre and sets it alight. She enters the flames and survives. When the fire has burned away, 3 newborn dragons cling to her, and the remaining Dothraki kneel.

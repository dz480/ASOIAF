## 1. Purpose

Collect loose endgame ideas **now**, without committing to a particular outcome, chronology, or overarching solution. After completing and studying the existing books' MNTs, especially those left unresolved by AFFC and ADWD, assess which ideas fit Martin's established narrative conditions and which must be revised, connected, rejected, or left open.

The eventual project is **not a replacement fanfiction**. It is a personal attempt to infer where GRRM's unfinished novels are heading by combining textual evidence, existing theories, television-derived signals, and independent interpretation, with their status and provenance made clear.

## 2. Provisional Narrative Timeline

**Status:** WORK IN PROGRESS / PROVISIONAL / UNCOMMITTED. **Version 5** updates the previous twenty-phase, six-front timeline with a provisional solution for the second Long Night. It is a hypothetical reconstruction connecting the combined AFFC/ADWD narrative endpoints to possible events in TWOW and ADOS, **not** confirmed GRRM plot.

**Reading convention:** The two tables are halves of **one master chronology**: Phase 0 through Phase 19, aligned by identical phase names. Each table presents three parallel narrative fronts to maintain legibility. Events sharing a phase are broadly overlapping, not necessarily simultaneous. Events marked as published endpoints are distinct from the hypothesised outcomes that follow. Earlier E01–E22 identifiers remain available in the MNT-linkage register, but this revision prioritises full event descriptions over repeated IDs.

**Endgame structure:** The struggle for the Iron Throne culminates in **King's Landing's destruction at 14**. The Wall falls at 16, and the final great war occurs **later, against the Others in the North at 18**. The working resolution combines Daenerys's **voluntary, fatal military action** with Bran and Sam's **hypothesised restoration of ancient protective magic**; it does not depend on killing a single Night King.

**Magical evidence boundary:** Northern tradition associates the Last Hero with the children of the forest, and the books demonstrate enchantments within the Wall, but **the identity of Sam's broken horn, Euron's ability to operate it, the existence of a renewable containment rite, the Others' motives and the terms of their retreat are unconfirmed**. The historical Pact on the Isle of Faces concerns the First Men and the children of the forest, not a documented treaty with the Others.

## 3. Open Chronological and Causal Questions

The timeline deliberately leaves several causal and chronological transitions unresolved. None of these questions has yet been reconciled with the completed AFFC and ADWD MNTs.

1. **Stannis and Shireen:** How does Stannis progress from victory at Winterfell to ordering Shireen's sacrifice, and what are the circumstances of his eventual downfall?
2. **The northern handover:** How do surviving northern houses, the free folk and remaining Baratheon forces come to accept Jon's military leadership? How do Jon's resurrection and Night's Watch obligations affect his position?
3. **Rickon and Stark succession:** Does Davos recover Rickon, when does Rickon return, and who holds political authority at Winterfell?
4. **The Greyjoys and Dragonbinder:** What becomes of Theon and Asha, and how does Victarion's mission in Meereen connect to Euron's purported control of a dragon?
5. **The ordering of the catastrophes:** Do King's Landing's destruction, Jon's mobilisation and the fall of the Wall occur in the proposed order, or do their timelines overlap differently?
6. **The defeat of the Others:** Can Bran and Sam reconstruct and renew the Wall's ancient protection after its destruction? What site, participants, vows and material conditions would such a rite require? Does Daenerys's fatal attack buy the needed time, and do the surviving Others retreat?
7. **Bran's accession:** Who recognises Bran as king, by what political or legal mechanism, and why is he accepted after Jon declines the throne?
8. **Cersei's purge:** How does she defeat geographically separated Tyrells and Faith leaders? Is it a single catastrophe or a succession of attacks? How do the people respond?
9. **Tommen and Myrcella:** Myrcella is still alive at the end of ADWD. How and when might Cersei lose both remaining children?
10. **Arya's Riverlands campaign:** What brings her home from Braavos, whom does she target, and how do her actions lead to Stoneheart?
11. **Stoneheart's end:** How do Arya, Jaime, Brienne, Podrick, Thoros and the surviving Brotherhood converge, and what makes Arya ending her mother's existence credible? Beric is already dead in book continuity.
12. **Arya's return north:** How does her travel align with Jon's restoration of Winterfell and preparations for the Others?
13. **Sam's broken horn:** Is it genuinely Joramun's Horn of Winter? How might Euron gain possession and activate a damaged artefact, and what evidence could distinguish magical ward-disruption from the destructive effect of dragonfire?
14. **The first Long Night:** What part of the Last Hero story is historical? Did the children of the forest establish the Wall as containment rather than victory by extinction? The much earlier First Men–children Pact must not be conflated with any conjectured arrangement involving the Others.
15. **The Others' motives and retreat:** Are they seeking conquest, favourable climatic conditions or something else? Would a renewed ward compel withdrawal, and what would make the outcome durable without requiring their extermination?
16. **Daenerys's final attack:** What observable battlefield emergency makes her fatal offensive indispensable to the defenders without inventing an unsupported blood-sacrifice rule?

17. **Jaime and Cersei:** How does Cersei remain in King's Landing after Aegon's victory? How can Jaime return without erasing his growth with Brienne, and how does the *valonqar* prophecy resolve?
18. **Jorah and Daenerys:** How does Jorah regain her trust before the fleet ambush, and what action makes his death essential to her survival?
19. **Benjen and Coldhands:** Can Bran establish Benjen's fate without conflating him with Coldhands?
20. **Melisandre and the ward:** What evidence would permit her fire magic to support Bran and Sam's restoration, and what does Shireen's death do to her faith?

These are **open research problems**, rather than missing scenes to be filled by assumption.

## 7. Working Principles

1. **Collect before reconciling.** New loose points can be added without rewriting the entire model.
2. **Preserve provenance.** Mark a borrowed fan theory, television inspiration, textual inference or personal synthesis when its origin is known; add precise citations later.
3. **Keep certainty honest.** A compelling scenario remains a hypothesis until it survives scrutiny against the novel MNTs.
4. **Respect literary causality.** Evaluate politics, institutions, character psychology, foreshadowing and magical constraints alongside plot resemblance.
5. **Revise freely.** Entries may be amended, split, merged, contradicted or discarded. Nothing in this file constitutes a final reconstruction.

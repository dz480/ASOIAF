# 1. Narrative Context

Beyond **the Wall**, 3 brothers of the **Night's Watch** have spent 9 days tracking wildling raiders through the **Haunted Forest**. **Will**, a former poacher whose skill as a woodsman made him a scout, serves under the young knight **Ser Waymar Royce** alongside the battle-hardened **Gared**. Will has located a wildling camp and reported 8 motionless people. As evening closes in, Gared wants to abandon the mission and return to the Wall; Waymar demands direct proof.

# 4. Narrative Overview

Deep in the **HAUNTED FOREST**, **Gared** urges his commander **Ser Waymar Royce** to turn back towards **the Wall**. The experienced ranger trusts **Will**'s report that 8 wildlings lie motionless beside a stream, but Waymar insists on seeing them. Will, a former poacher accustomed to silent movement, shares Gared's fear of the worsening cold. Waymar points out that the recent weather should not have killed 8 properly clothed adults and orders the party forward.

They reach a wooded ridge by moonlight. Waymar leaves Gared guarding their horses, rejects his request for a fire and commands Will to lead on. From beneath a sentinel tree, Will discovers that the wildlings have disappeared, leaving their camp and a discarded axe behind. Waymar demands that he climb to search for movement. Hidden above, Will sees pale figures gliding almost noiselessly among the trees.

An **Other**, clothed in shifting pale armour and carrying a translucent sword, confronts Waymar. The young knight stands his ground despite the supernatural cold, while more Others gather around him. Their blades clash with an agonising sound; Waymar is wounded, then his steel sword shatters. The watchers converge and kill him as Will hides.

Hours later, Will climbs down to retrieve the broken blade as proof. The mutilated knight rises before him, 1 eye shining blue, and closes his icy hands around Will's throat.

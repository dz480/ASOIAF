# 1. Narrative Context

The long summer continues across **Westeros**, 15 years after **Robert Baratheon** overthrew the **Targaryen** dynasty. His closest surviving friend from the rebellion, **Eddard Stark**, governs **WINTERFELL** and **THE NORTH**, far from the royal court in **KING'S LANDING**. Ned's household remains rooted in his marriage to **Catelyn Stark**, their children, and the responsibilities inherited from generations of northern lords.

The recent death of **Jon Arryn**, who fostered both men and subsequently served as Robert's Hand, has left the king without his principal adviser. Robert travels north with **Cersei Lannister**, their children and a formidable royal entourage. Although the rebellion established Robert's kingship, the old conflicts between the Starks, Lannisters and Targaryens remain embedded in their personal histories. Ned's attachment to his family and northern duties stands against the obligations of his friendship with the king.

# 4. Narrative Overview

**CSRM chapters (publication order):** [Ch. 4](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-4) · [Ch. 12](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-12) · [Ch. 16](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-16) · [Ch. 20](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-20) · [Ch. 25](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-25) · [Ch. 27](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-27) · [Ch. 30](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-30) · [Ch. 33](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-33) · [Ch. 35](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-35) · [Ch. 39](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-39) · [Ch. 43](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-43) · [Ch. 45](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-45) · [Ch. 47](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-47) · [Ch. 49](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-49) · [Ch. 58](ROM_CSRM_AGOT.md#agot-ch-58)

**Robert Baratheon** arrives at **WINTERFELL** with his royal entourage, and **Eddard Stark** accompanies his old friend into the family crypts to honour **Lyanna Stark**. Robert offers Ned the vacant Handship and proposes a marriage between **Joffrey** and **Sansa**. Reluctantly accepting, Ned leaves the North with his daughters. During the journey, Robert's hostility towards the surviving Targaryens exposes their longstanding disagreement over the killing of children. At **Darry**, a confrontation involving **Arya**, Joffrey and the butcher's boy **Mycah** ends with Mycah dead, Arya's direwolf **Nymeria** missing, and Ned forced to kill Sansa's innocent direwolf, **Lady**.

Arriving in **KING'S LANDING**, Ned encounters the small council of **Petyr Baelish**, **Varys**, **Grand Maester Pycelle** and **Renly Baratheon**, discovers the Crown's enormous debts, and begins examining **Jon Arryn**'s final activities. Littlefinger secretly brings him to **Catelyn**, who reports the attempt on Bran's life and presents the Valyrian steel dagger allegedly belonging to **Tyrion Lannister**. Ned questions Pycelle about Jon's illness, obtains the book of noble genealogies that Jon requested, and follows leads involving Robert's illegitimate children. At **Tobho Mott**'s forge, he encounters **Gendry**, whose black hair and resemblance to Robert become important evidence.

The Hand's tournament brings renewed danger. Ned examines the death of **Ser Hugh of the Vale**, watches **Loras Tyrell** defeat **Gregor Clegane**, and sees **Sandor Clegane** intervene when his brother attacks the young knight. Varys subsequently tells Ned that Jon Arryn was poisoned with the tears of Lys, although the identity of the murderer remains unresolved. Meanwhile, Robert's decision to assassinate the pregnant **Daenerys Targaryen** causes Ned to surrender his office. Littlefinger later takes him to another of Robert's illegitimate children, **Barra**, but the return journey ends in Jaime's ambush. **Jory**, **Wyl** and **Heward** are killed, and Ned suffers a shattered leg.

During Ned's recovery, Robert reinstates him as Hand. Reports of Gregor Clegane's devastation of the riverlands then reach the **Iron Throne**, where Ned commissions **Beric Dondarrion** and other men to execute royal justice. His genealogical investigation finally reveals that Robert's acknowledged children by Cersei lack the characteristic Baratheon appearance because Jaime is their father. Ned confronts Cersei privately in the **Red Keep** godswood, offering her an opportunity to escape with her children before he tells Robert.

Robert returns from hunting mortally wounded by a boar. Ned writes the king's final instructions, substituting a reference to Robert's lawful heir for the intended reference to Joffrey, while Robert unknowingly confirms Ned's regency. Refusing Renly's proposal to seize Cersei's children, Ned prepares a message for **Stannis** and seeks Littlefinger's assistance in securing the **City Watch**. He also arranges for Arya and Sansa to return to Winterfell. When Robert dies, however, Cersei rejects his instructions, Joffrey occupies the Iron Throne, and Janos Slynt's gold cloaks turn on Ned's guards. Littlefinger personally betrays Ned, ending his attempt to establish lawful succession.

Imprisoned beneath the Red Keep, Ned struggles with his injured leg, hunger, isolation and uncertainty over his family. Varys eventually visits disguised as a gaoler. He reports that Arya escaped capture, Sansa remains under Cersei's control, and **Robb** has raised an army, while the Lannisters face increasing danger from Robert's surviving brothers. Varys proposes that Ned confess to treason, recognise Joffrey and abandon Stannis's cause in exchange for the possibility of taking the black. Ned initially rejects the dishonourable confession, but Varys invokes Sansa's vulnerability. Their conversation ends with Ned confronting a decision whose consequences extend far beyond his own imprisonment.

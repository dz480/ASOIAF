# 1. Narrative Context

**Catelyn Stark**, born a **Tully of Riverrun** and married to **Eddard Stark**, is Lady of **Winterfell** and mother to the Stark children. She has made a home in the North without fully sharing her husband's faith in its old gods. After Eddard executes a terrified Night's Watch deserter, she finds him cleaning **Ice** beside the godswood's heart tree. **Jon Arryn**, the foster father of Eddard and King Robert, has died, while the king and his household are travelling north. Catelyn values her family's present security, yet Jon's death and the king's approaching visit threaten to draw her husband, children and northern household into the dangers of southern politics.

# 4. Narrative Overview


In the **Winterfell** godswood, **Catelyn Stark** tells **Eddard** that **Jon Arryn** has died and **King Robert** is coming north. A hidden letter from her sister **Lysa** subsequently alleges that the Lannisters murdered Jon. Catelyn and **Maester Luwin** urge Ned to accept Robert’s offer of the Handship so he can investigate and protect his friend. Ned agrees to leave for **King’s Landing** with their daughters and Bran, while Catelyn remains to counsel **Robb**. Their family is already dividing when Bran falls from a tower and is left unconscious.

Catelyn refuses to leave Bran’s bedside, neglecting her household and younger son. While a fire distracts the castle, an intruder enters Bran’s room to murder him. Catelyn seizes his dagger and is badly wounded; Bran’s direwolf **Summer** kills the man. The expensive Valyrian-steel weapon convinces Catelyn that the attack was arranged. Suspecting that Bran was pushed, she leaves Robb in charge and secretly sails south with **Ser Rodrik Cassel**. In the capital, **Varys** finds her and brings her to **Petyr Baelish**, who claims that **Tyrion Lannister** won the dagger from him in a wager. Catelyn treats the allegation as an important lead, though it remains his unverified account.

Returning north, Catelyn encounters Tyrion unexpectedly at the crowded **crossroads inn**. Once he recognises her, she invokes the loyalty of nearby riverland men and arrests him, taking her captive east to the **Vale** instead of Winterfell. The mountain journey is dangerous; the ascent to the **Eyrie**, guided by **Mya Stone**, leaves Catelyn exhausted. She expects aid from Lysa but finds her sister terrified of provoking the Lannisters and devoted to protecting young **Robert Arryn**. Lysa blames Tyrion for Jon’s death, although Catelyn cannot establish his involvement. **Maester Colemon** also describes a plan by Jon to foster the boy with **Stannis**, troubling Catelyn’s understanding of events. Lysa demands a public trial by combat. **Bronn** defeats and kills **Ser Vardis Egen**, freeing Tyrion. Disgusted by Lysa’s refusal to assist Riverrun, Catelyn’s uncle **Brynden Tully** leaves the Vale with her.

At **Moat Cailin**, Catelyn reunites with Robb and his assembled northern host. The Lannisters are invading the riverlands; Catelyn’s father **Hoster** is gravely ill, and her brother **Edmure** struggles to defend Riverrun. **Sansa’s** letter persuades Catelyn that the girl is being coerced. Robb’s growing authority impresses her, but she fears his inexperience. She helps him choose **Roose Bolton** to command the infantry against **Tywin Lannister**, while Robb, accompanied by **Theon Greyjoy**, the cavalry and Catelyn, moves toward **Jaime’s** siege of Riverrun.

At the **Twins**, **Walder Frey** bars their path and demands guarantees for his allegiance. Catelyn negotiates a passage, Frey soldiers, **Olyvar Frey** as Robb’s squire, Robb’s future marriage to a Frey daughter and a proposed match for the missing Arya. The cavalry crosses and rides toward Riverrun while Bolton’s foot draws Tywin away. In the **Whispering Wood**, Catelyn waits as Robb’s men ambush Jaime’s cavalry. Theon and **Greatjon Umber** return with Jaime captive, but the victory costs the lives of two sons of **Rickard Karstark** and **Daryn Hornwood**, killed as Jaime tried to reach Robb. Catelyn reminds her son that they have won a battle, not the war.

Catelyn finally returns to **Riverrun**, now relieved, and reunites with Edmure and her dying father. **Eddard’s execution** has left her grieving and fearful for the daughters she cannot protect. As the lords debate **Renly**, **Stannis** and possible peace, she urges an end to the killing and hopes to recover her children. Greatjon instead calls for an independent northern king; Rickard Karstark, **Maege Mormont** and the riverlords kneel to Robb. Catelyn’s effort to keep the family together ends with her eldest son acclaimed **King in the North**, committed to a wider war whose outcome remains uncertain.

Suspicions remain character knowledge rather than unquestioned narrative fact.

> **Roll Call source census — Applies to: [5. The [AGOT Master Character Map](https://github.com/dz480/ASOIAF/blob/main/ROM_MCM_AGOT.md) informs Significance tier checking but cannot replace primary-text presence verification.

# 1. Narrative Context

**Sansa Stark**, elder daughter of **Eddard Stark** and **Catelyn Stark**, has grown up at **Winterfell** with the manners, songs and noble marriages expected of a highborn girl. Her younger sister **Arya** rejects many of those expectations, whereas Sansa values them. **King Robert Baratheon** has named Eddard his Hand and arranged Sansa's betrothal to **Prince Joffrey**, son of **Queen Cersei Lannister**. The royal company is travelling south through the **riverlands** toward **King's Landing**, taking both Stark daughters away from their northern home. Sansa has brought her gentle direwolf **Lady**, and anticipates life at court as a future queen; political hostility between their families has not yet visibly disturbed her confidence in the match.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At the **RUBY FORD**, **Sansa Stark** quarrels with **Arya** over the queen's invitation and meets **Barristan Selmy**, **Renly Baratheon** and the tongueless **Ilyn Payne**. **Joffrey** takes her riding and gives her wine before discovering Arya practising swordplay with **Mycah**. He draws steel on the frightened boy; Arya hits him and **Nymeria** bites his sword arm. Arya throws his sword into the river, and Joffrey contemptuously rejects Sansa's help. In the aftermath, Mycah and Sansa's direwolf **Lady** are killed, although Sansa continues to favour the prince.

At the **HAND'S TOURNEY** in **KING'S LANDING**, Sansa sees **Gregor Clegane** fatally lance a young knight and **Loras Tyrell** present her with a red rose. **Petyr Baelish** remarks on her resemblance to her mother. Joffrey treats her affectionately at the feast, but sends **Sandor Clegane** to escort her home. In the dark, Sandor tells her how Gregor burned his face as a child, bitterly condemns knights and threatens her if she repeats the story.

Later, Sansa and Arya argue over Joffrey and their father's guards. **Eddard Stark**, injured and increasingly fearful for their safety, orders both daughters back to **Winterfell** and intends to break Sansa's engagement. She protests vehemently. Her later account reveals that she defied his instruction and informed Cersei of the planned departure.

After Ned's arrest, Sansa and **Jeyne Poole** are confined in the **Red Keep**. **Cersei**, **Varys**, **Pycelle** and Littlefinger tell Sansa her father betrayed Joffrey. Littlefinger assumes custody of Jeyne, whom Sansa does not see again. Cersei promises that the marriage may survive if Sansa proves her loyalty and makes her write letters to **Catelyn**, **Robb** and other relatives asking them to submit to the new king.

At Joffrey's court, Sansa witnesses honours granted to his supporters, Barristan's dismissal from the Kingsguard and Sandor's appointment to his place. She kneels before the **Iron Throne**, begs mercy for her father and accepts Joffrey's condition that Ned confess and acknowledge him as king. Her hopes collapse when Joffrey orders Ned's execution.

In the final chapter, Sansa lies grieving in her chambers. Joffrey forces her into public attendance, and **Meryn Trant** strikes her for defiance. After watching Joffrey issue cruel judgments, she must climb the castle wall and look at the tarred heads of Ned and **Septa Mordane**. Sansa answers Joffrey's threats with a reference to Robb taking his head; Meryn beats her again. For a moment she considers pushing Joffrey from the parapet, before Sandor steps between them and gently wipes the blood from her face.

# 1. Narrative Context

After **Khal Drogo**'s death and the breakup of his khalasar, **Daenerys Targaryen** has hatched three dragons from the eggs given her by **Illyrio Mopatis**. She remains with a small band of loyal **Dothraki**, including bloodriders **Jhogo**, **Aggo** and **Rakharo**, handmaids **Irri**, **Jhiqui** and **Doreah**, and the exiled knight **Jorah Mormont**, whom she has named her first Queensguard. The rival khalasars could overwhelm her weakened followers and seize the hatchlings. With few horses and little food, Daenerys must find a route away from her enemies while keeping the dragons and her surviving people alive.

# 4. Narrative Overview

Guided by the red comet, **Daenerys Targaryen** takes her weakened **Dothraki** east across the **Red Waste**, away from the larger khalasars. People and horses die as water and food disappear; she tends the feverish **Doreah** until her death. The three hatchlings prosper on charred meat, and Daenerys names them **Drogon**, **Rhaegal** and **Viserion** for her husband and brothers. Her followers reach the deserted, white-walled **Vaes Tolorro**, where gardens and wells allow them to recover. **Jorah Mormont** recounts how love for **Lynesse Hightower** led him into debt, slave trading and exile. Daenerys dispatches **Aggo**, **Rakharo** and **Jhogo** in separate directions. Jhogo returns from **Qarth** with three visitors: the wealthy **Xaro Xhoan Daxos**, warlock **Pyat Pree** and masked **Quaithe**.

They escort Daenerys into Qarth, and Xaro lodges her entire company in his palace. She sends Jorah to investigate the harbour and receives **Quhuru Mo**, captain of the *Cinnamon Wind*, who reports that **Robert Baratheon** is dead and Westeros is divided. Daenerys hopes to exploit the rival claimants, but Jorah warns that restoration will still require ships, soldiers and allies. By the time she returns from petitioning the **Pureborn**, she has spent gifts and bribes for nothing: they will supply no fleet. Xaro offers marriage but evades her requests for ships; Jorah reveals a Qartheen marriage custom allowing each spouse to demand a single possession, which could cost her a dragon.

In the bazaar, Daenerys sees a firemage climb a ladder of flame. Quaithe attributes his newly strengthened power to the return of dragons and urges Daenerys to leave Qarth. Finding no conventional supporters, Daenerys visits Pyat Pree's **House of the Undying**, enters alone with Drogon and drinks **shade of the evening**. A twisting passage reveals scenes of death, childhood, Targaryen history and possible futures; the images do not become established events merely because she sees them. The **Undying** speak of three heads, fires, mounts and treasons, then close around her. Drogon burns their pulsing blue heart, setting the chamber alight, and Daenerys escapes to **Jorah**, Jhogo and Rakharo, who overpower the knife-wielding Pyat.

**Xaro** demands a dragon in exchange for ships and then orders Daenerys out of his house; she also learns of plots for revenge among the warlocks. At Qarth's quays, a stranger presents her with a jewelled box containing a venomous **manticore**. An elderly Westerosi, **Arstan Whitebeard**, knocks it away and saves her. He and the formidable former pit fighter **Strong Belwas** reveal that **Illyrio Mopatis** has sent them to escort Daenerys to **Pentos** with three ships. She accepts the passage for her followers, horses and dragons and orders the vessels renamed *Vhagar*, *Meraxes* and *Balerion*.

# 1. Narrative Context

**Catelyn Stark**, widowed by **Eddard Stark**'s execution, has helped her eldest son **Robb** gather the northern host and win battles against the **Lannisters**. Robb's lords have acclaimed him **King in the North**, bringing the question of independence into the war already consuming the **Riverlands**. His great prize is the captive **Jaime Lannister**, whom Catelyn regards as a possible means of recovering her daughters **Sansa** and **Arya** from royal custody. Her father **Hoster Tully** lies gravely ill at **Riverrun**, while her brother **Edmure** struggles to defend his father's lands. Rival royal claims are gathering strength in the south, and Catelyn must reconcile the new demands of a son's kingship with the threatened survival of her remaining family.

# 4. Narrative Overview

In the **Great Hall of Riverrun**, **Catelyn Stark** watches her crowned son **Robb** receive the captive **Ser Cleos Frey**, whom he proposes to send to **Cersei Lannister** under a peace banner. Robb's terms demand the safe return of **Sansa** and **Arya**, the remains of **Ned Stark** and the sword **Ice**, a prisoner exchange and independence for the **North** and allied **Riverlands**. **Greatjon Umber** applauds; **Rickard Karstark**, whose sons Jaime killed, walks out. Catelyn warns that Cersei will want **Jaime** rather than lesser captives, but Robb refuses to release the Kingslayer even to recover his sisters. The quarrel exposes the competing demands of a king's standing among his lords and a mother's need to protect her children. Robb also insists on sending **Theon Greyjoy** to **Balon** to negotiate an ironborn alliance, despite Catelyn's warning that Theon is worth more as a hostage. She visits her failing father **Hoster Tully** and consults her uncle **Brynden**, whose scouts report that **Tywin Lannister** is exploiting the ravaged Riverlands and deliberately trying to provoke Robb into attacking fortified **Harrenhal**. Catelyn sees another possibility: using the army of **Renly Baratheon** to divert Tywin.

Robb sends Catelyn south as his envoy. Travelling with **Hallis Mollen**, **Ser Wendel Manderly** and other Stark guards, she crosses territory affected by war and reaches Renly's enormous camp at **Bitterbridge**, where southern lords are holding a splendid melee. She watches a formidable knight in blue armour defeat **Loras Tyrell**; the victor is **Brienne of Tarth**, mocked for her appearance but rewarded with a place in Renly's **Rainbow Guard**. Renly receives Catelyn beside his young queen **Margaery Tyrell** and offers justice for Ned, but is unwilling to acknowledge Robb as a separate king. In conversation he argues that popularity, resources and the support of the **Reach** make him a better prospective monarch than his elder brother Stannis. Before any agreement can be struck, a messenger announces that Stannis has moved against **Storm's End**, and Renly takes his forces toward his ancestral seat.

Near Storm's End, Catelyn attends the formal parley between the Baratheon brothers, accompanied by Hallis. **Stannis**, bearing a fiery stag device carried by **Melisandre**, insists that his elder-brother claim is lawful and alleges that **Joffrey**, **Tommen** and **Myrcella** are the children of Cersei and her brother Jaime. **Renly** responds with mockery, displays a peach and claims that superior numbers and his supporters' choice should decide the crown. Catelyn urges them to stop a conflict that would profit the Lannisters, but neither will yield. Back in Renly's pavilion, **Mathis Rowan** counsels delay while **Randyll Tarly** urges decisive battle. Renly resolves to attack the much smaller force at dawn and forbids Catelyn to depart until she has witnessed the outcome; **Ser Robar Royce** escorts her to a village sept, where she prays through the night for her children and the war's victims.

Returning before dawn, Catelyn enters Renly's lit pavilion as **Brienne** arms him and his commanders complete their plans. Catelyn has begun to suspect that Bran's fall followed his discovery of the Lannister twins together, and makes one final appeal for the brothers to settle their quarrel. A cold gust opens the tent flap. Catelyn glimpses a shadow whose apparent sword cuts through the metal at Renly's throat, killing him in Brienne's arms. **Ser Emmon Cuy** and **Ser Robar Royce** rush in and accuse Brienne. She takes Renly's sword to defend herself, while Catelyn argues that neither of them killed him and persuades Robar to protect their escape. Catelyn knocks Emmon down with a brazier; she and Brienne cut through the tent, leaving it burning and the army falling into confusion. Brienne swears vengeance against Stannis, whom Catelyn suspects of responsibility, although she cannot explain the shadow's workings.

The fugitives travel back toward Riverrun. They encounter **Martyn Rivers** and his Frey riders, who report **Robb**'s victory at **Oxcross**, and learn that **Tywin** has marched from Harrenhal. Brienne asks to leave so she can attempt to kill Stannis. Catelyn urges her to serve the living rather than die pursuing vengeance; Brienne instead vows to serve Catelyn, receiving a promise that her old oath will not be dismissed. Approaching Riverrun they see hanged men outside its walls. **Edmure Tully** reports that Cleos returned with apparent peace envoys who secretly tried to release Jaime. The prisoners killed guards; Jaime briefly escaped his cell and killed additional defenders before being recaptured. The false envoys have been executed and Cleos detained. Edmure has admitted frightened smallfolk inside the fortress and called his bannermen to protect the river crossings. Catelyn visits **Hoster**, now struggling to recognise her, and receives **Ned's** bones, delivered by the Lannisters but not accompanied by Ice. She entrusts them to 2 **Silent Sisters** and orders **Hallis Mollen** to escort them home to the crypts beneath Winterfell.

Edmure rides to defend the **Red Fork**, leaving Catelyn at Riverrun with Brienne. Their conversations move between Renly, the perils of childbirth and family, and **Edric Storm**, Robert's acknowledged bastard at Storm's End; information about the boy leads Catelyn to reconsider what made Bran dangerous to the Lannisters. **Cleos** explains the royal response to Robb's terms, including a conditional offer to exchange Sansa and Arya for Jaime, but the surrounding conditions and trustworthiness of the offer remain contested. **Rymund the Rhymer** sings as the castle awaits news of the fighting. Reports from the fords describe Edmure defeating successive attempted crossings by Tywin's forces; men celebrate, yet Catelyn examines a map and remains troubled by the direction of the enemy's march and the consequences of Edmure's victories.

In her final selected chapter, Catelyn has received a raven from **Rodrik Cassel** saying that **Theon** seized Winterfell and that her sons **Bran** and **Rickon** were killed. Her information comes from the letter, not an enacted northern scene; she accepts the report as devastating truth. She confesses her grief to **Brienne**, visits her dying father and then descends to **Jaime Lannister**'s dungeon cell after midnight, bringing wine and dismissing the gaoler. Jaime admits that he and **Cersei** are lovers, that he fathered her children, and that he deliberately threw Bran from the tower to conceal the discovery. He denies having sent the catspaw who later tried to kill Bran and disputes Catelyn's accusations against Tyrion; the question of who arranged that later attack remains unsettled. Jaime describes the violent deaths of **Rickard** and **Brandon Stark** under **Aerys II**, and argues bitterly that the vows of knighthood and kingsguard service can contradict one another. When he taunts Catelyn over **Littlefinger** and Jon Snow, she calls **Brienne** into the cell and asks for her sword. The chapter ends before she discloses what she intends to do with it.

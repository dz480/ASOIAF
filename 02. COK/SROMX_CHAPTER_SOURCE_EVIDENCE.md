## Source-confirmed follow-up corrections

1. **Arya I, Ch. 1:** The boy later called Gendry is described as **the Bull** in the chapter and is not yet called *Gendry* by name. The [Arya SROMX](SROMX_COK_POV_ARYA.md) timeline has been updated to preserve the chapter-local name while clarifying the later identity.
2. **Tyrion IX / Tyrion XI / Sansa VIII, Ch. 41 / 49 / 65:** Tyrion IX's High Septon dies in the city riot. The **new High Septon** is directly enacted during the Kingsguard ceremony in Ch. 49, and the successor presides at the court in Ch. 65. The earlier [Tyrion SROMX](SROMX_COK_POV_TYRION.md) correction now has a matching [Sansa SROMX](SROMX_COK_POV_SANSA.md) identity clarification. Both officeholders remain titled, unnamed individuals under the named-only MCM policy.

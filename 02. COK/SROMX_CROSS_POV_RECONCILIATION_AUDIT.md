# COK SROMX — Cross-POV Reconciliation Audit

**Review date:** 14 Oct 2026  
**Scope:** All 10 published COK POV SROMX records, COK ROM MCM (Satisfactory for Use — Priority Verified), COK ROM CSRM, supplied COK RTE schedule and SROMX v11.29.  
**Outcome:** First-pass canonical reconciliation applied. **Outstanding:** source-based MCM roster adjudication for 37 individually identified SROMX entries absent from the current 263-character MCM roster, plus a more exhaustive scene-level cross-POV fact audit. This document is a maintenance register, not a new literary synopsis.

## Applied changes

- Corrected **17 conflicting significance markers** in 4 POV records: Arya (3), Catelyn (3), Daenerys (7), Davos (4).
- Recalculated displayed Roll Call tier counts for Arya, Catelyn, Daenerys, Davos and Tyrion; retained census totals and preserved original source-census entry indices.
- Updated all 10 SROMX documents' outdated references to the former provisional MCM, now **Satisfactory for Use — Priority Verified**. This description does not imply that every excluded SROMX participant has been adjudicated into its roster.
- Divided oversized character tables into source-grounded subgroups for Arya, Bran, Catelyn, Jon, Sansa, Theon and Tyrion, retaining five-column schema, character order and original index numbering. The Cressen, Daenerys and Davos Roll Calls already met the six-row layout requirement.
- Verified COK RTE chapter assignments and scoped POV chapter counts. Compared the displayed dates in SROMX timeline entries against corresponding CSRM estimates. All checkable date anchors matched the CSRM's *fan-reconstructed, noncanonical* dates. A few multi-day SROMX ranges begin at the same CSRM date and are not inherently contradictory.
- Confirmed important multi-identity mappings for significance comparison without exposing later identities in early POV narrative text: **Reek = Ramsay Snow**, **Arstan Whitebeard = Barristan Selmy**, and standard name variants (e.g. Ser Mandon Moore, Aeron Damphair, the Blackfish).

# 1. Narrative Context

At **Castle Black**, **Jon Snow** has sworn himself to the **Night's Watch**, becoming steward and trusted attendant to Lord Commander **Jeor Mormont**. He remains troubled by **Eddard Stark's** death, **Robb's** war and the unresolved disappearance of his uncle **Benjen Stark**. Dead men have risen within the Watch's own walls, while rangers vanish beyond **the Wall** and reports suggest that **Mance Rayder** is gathering the free folk. Mormont prepares a major ranging to investigate these threats. The coming expedition will put Jon's vows, his loyalty to his companions and his understanding of the northern wilderness under pressure.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At **Castle Black**, **Jon Snow** finds **Samwell Tarly** engrossed in the library's maps and chronicles. **Jeor Mormont** is preparing a Great Ranging beyond **the Wall**, and Jon must help his frightened friend face the expedition. **Donal Noye** warns him about the Baratheon brothers and the conflict spreading across the realm. The Old Bear recounts how **Maester Aemon** once declined a crown rather than abandon his vows, then asks Jon what he will do now that **Robb** has been crowned King in the North. Jon insists that, however troubled, he will keep his own vows.

The ranging passes deserted wildling villages. At **Whitetree**, beneath an ancient heart tree, Jon discovers human bones and sees how fearful the Watch has become. **Dywen** and the other foresters find scant game, while **Sam** makes himself useful handling ravens. After prolonged rain, the men reach **Craster's Keep**, where Craster offers grudging shelter. Jon witnesses the degradation of Craster's wives and daughters. A young woman named **Gilly** asks him to take her unborn child away, fearing what Craster does to his sons. Sam also tries to help her. Jon brings the matter to Mormont, but the Lord Commander refuses to divert his mission. Craster supplies news that **Mance Rayder** is gathering a vast force in the **Frostfangs**.

The Watch rides onward to the hillfort called the **Fist of the First Men**. Mormont sees its old ringwall as defensible ground; **Thoren Smallwood** argues over scouting and the uncertainty of Mance's position. **Ghost** repeatedly resists entering the camp, then leads Jon beneath the hill to a freshly buried bundle. Inside are dragonglass daggers, points and arrowheads, an old damaged warhorn and a black cloak of the Night's Watch. Later, the legendary **Qhorin Halfhand** arrives from the Shadow Tower, reporting that Mance has assembled rival free folk leaders and that scouts must enter the mountains to discover his intentions. Qhorin selects Jon for his party.

In the **Skirling Pass**, **Stonesnake** and Jon climb a treacherous cliff to silence a wildling watchfire. They discover 3 sentries rather than 2: the rangers kill 2 men, while Jon captures **Ygritte**, a young spearwife. She tells him the wildling tale of **Bael the Bard** and the daughter of Winterfell, challenging the version of Stark history Jon knows. Qhorin has no means to guard a captive and leaves Jon to decide what must be done. Jon raises **Longclaw**, but lets her go.

As the scouts retreat, Qhorin questions Jon's mercy. A wolf dream then carries Jon into Ghost's perceptions and towards a strange weirwood-associated encounter linked to **Bran**; Ghost is subsequently found wounded from an attack by a hostile eagle. Pursuit closes behind them. **Squire Dalbridge** stays at a narrow mountain passage to delay the hunters. The party hears horns behind, and **Ebben** is sent to warn Mormont. When **Stonesnake's** horse breaks its leg, Qhorin orders him to escape alone on foot and report Jon's discoveries if he can. Only Qhorin and Jon remain together, travelling through cold, hunger and fear.

Near a rocky cave, Qhorin and Jon recite the vows of the Watch. Qhorin requires Jon to remember his duty, even if he must appear to abandon it. **Rattleshirt** arrives with armed free folk and **Ragwyle**, who displays Ebben's severed head as proof of the pursuit's cost. Jon is told to turn against the Halfhand. Qhorin draws his sword and presses the duel; Ghost bites his leg, enabling Jon to land the fatal thrust. The free folk burn Qhorin's body. Ygritte persuades them to keep Jon alive despite Rattleshirt's distrust, and tells him that Mance and his army have begun their march south towards the Wall.

## SEQ 1: BANNERS BENEATH THE COMET
**New Kings Raise Their Claims As Scattered Survivors Find Their Allegiances**

<a id="cok-csrm-prologue"></a>
### Prologue: Cressen

[SROMX | Maester Cressen | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_CRESSEN.md) · Prologue POV only

**≈1 Feb 299 AC**  
*Dragonstone*  
**CRESSEN** · **DAVOS** · **MELISANDRE** · **SALLADHOR SAAN** · **SELYSE FLORENT** · **STANNIS**

**Maester Cressen** sees the red comet and fears **Melisandre**'s growing hold over **Stannis Baratheon**. Stannis refuses his warnings and turns to the red priestess; Cressen tries to poison Melisandre at the feast, but she survives and he dies from his own poisoned wine.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-1"></a>
### Ch. 1: Arya I

**POV SROMX:** [Arya Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_ARYA.md) (through Ch. 64).

**≈17 Jan 299 AC**  
*Kingsroad south of King's Landing*  
**ARYA** · **GENDRY** · **JAQEN**

Disguised as a boy called Arry, **Arya Stark** joins **Yoren**'s recruits with **Gendry**, Hot Pie and Lommy. She conceals her identity amid hostile companions and dreams of reaching **Jon Snow** at the Wall.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-2"></a>
### Ch. 2: Sansa I

[SROMX | Sansa Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_SANSA.md) (complete POV through Ch. 65)

**≈10 Feb 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**SANSA** · **ARYS OAKHEART** · **BRONN** · **DONTOS** · **JOFFREY** · **MERYN TRANT** · **SANDOR** · **SHAGGA** · **TYRION**

On **Joffrey**'s nameday, **Sansa** witnesses his cruelty towards Ser Dontos and persuades him to spare the drunken knight by making him a fool. **Sandor Clegane** intervenes as the king's violence escalates, and **Tyrion** arrives to assume his father's authority.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-3"></a>
### Ch. 3: Tyrion I

**≈10 Feb 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**TYRION** · **BRONN** · **CERSEI LANNISTER** · **PYCELLE** · **JANOS SLYNT** · **LITTLEFINGER** · **SHAE** · **VARYS**

**Tyrion** presents **Tywin**'s letter appointing him acting Hand, displacing **Cersei**'s control of the small council. He assesses **Pycelle**, **Varys** and **Littlefinger**, and secretly brings **Shae** to the capital despite his father's prohibition.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-4"></a>
### Ch. 4: Bran I

**≈7 Feb 299 AC**  
*Winterfell* · *Wolfswood (wolf dream)*  
**BRAN** · **LUWIN** · **OSHA**

**Bran** governs a tense Winterfell in **Robb**'s absence as the Frey wards settle in. His bond with **Summer** produces vivid wolf dreams, and **Osha** warns that dark changes are coming from beyond the Wall.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-5"></a>
### Ch. 5: Arya II

**POV SROMX:** [Arya Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_ARYA.md) (through Ch. 64).

**≈1 Feb 299 AC**  
*Kingsroad* · *inn near the Gods Eye*  
**ARYA** · **GENDRY** · **JAQEN**

**Arya** and **Yoren** protect **Gendry** when gold cloaks intercept their party and demand the apprentice. Yoren refuses to hand him over, revealing that the danger following the recruits is political rather than accidental.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-6"></a>
### Ch. 6: Jon I

**≈20 Mar 299 AC**  
*Castle Black*  
**JON** · **MORMONT** · **SAMWELL TARLY**

**Jon Snow** prepares for the Great Ranging as **Jeor Mormont** gathers the Watch. Their conversation tests Jon's loyalty to his vows against his anger over his father's death and his brother **Robb**'s war.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-7"></a>
### Ch. 7: Catelyn I

**≈18 Feb 299 AC**  
*Riverrun*  
**CATELYN** · **BRYNDEN TULLY** · **CLEOS FREY** · **EDMURE TULLY** · **GREATJON** · **RICKARD KARSTARK** · **ROBB STARK** · **THEON**

**Catelyn** sees **Robb** crowned and commanding a war that leaves the Riverlands devastated. **Cleos Frey** brings Lannister peace terms, but the Stark cause cannot accept them; Catelyn resolves to seek an alliance with **Renly Baratheon**.

## SEQ 2: ROADS INTO DARKNESS
**Tyrion Consolidates The Capital While Arya, Davos And Theon Face Opposing Powers**

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-8"></a>
### Ch. 8: Tyrion II

**≈24 Feb 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**TYRION** · **BRONN** · **BYWATER** · **JANOS SLYNT** · **VARYS**

**Tyrion** arrests and exiles Gold Cloak commander **Janos Slynt**, replacing him with **Jacelyn Bywater**. He gains control of the city watch while using **Bronn** and **Varys** to tighten his hold over the capital.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-9"></a>
### Ch. 9: Arya III

**POV SROMX:** [Arya Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_ARYA.md) (through Ch. 64).

**≈21 Feb 299 AC**  
*Riverlands* · *western shore of the Gods Eye*  
**ARYA** · **GENDRY** · **JAQEN**

**Arya**'s party leaves the kingsroad and crosses increasingly ravaged country. With **Yoren** determined to avoid soldiers, the refugees see signs of slaughter while seeking a viable passage north.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-10"></a>
### Ch. 10: Davos I

**≈3 Mar 299 AC**  
*Dragonstone*  
**DAVOS** · **MELISANDRE** · **SALLADHOR SAAN** · **SELYSE FLORENT** · **STANNIS**

**Davos Seaworth** watches **Melisandre** burn the statues of the Seven as **Stannis** claims the flaming sword Lightbringer. Stannis issues letters denouncing **Joffrey**'s parentage; Davos questions the new faith but remains loyal.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-11"></a>
### Ch. 11: Theon I

**≈25 Mar 299 AC**  
*Pyke*  
**THEON** · **BALON GREYJOY**

**Theon Greyjoy** returns to his father's islands and learns that **Balon** neither trusts him nor intends to ally with **Robb Stark**. The proposed ironborn campaign targets the North, forcing Theon to choose between his foster brother and blood family.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-12"></a>
### Ch. 12: Daenerys I

**≈27 Jan–22 Apr 299 AC**  
*Red Waste* · *Vaes Tolorro*  
**DAENERYS** · **JORAH MORMONT** · **PYAT PREE** · **XARO**

**Daenerys** leads her starving remnant across the red desert, loses companions and finds refuge in the ruined city of Vaes Tolorro. Her riders return with news of **Qarth**, whose emissaries **Xaro**, Pyat Pree and Quaithe come seeking her dragons.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-13"></a>
### Ch. 13: Jon II

**≈21 Apr 299 AC**  
*Haunted Forest* · *Whitetree*  
**JON** · **MORMONT** · **SAMWELL TARLY**

On the Great Ranging, **Jon** and **Jeor Mormont** discover abandoned villages and disturbing signs of the free folk's flight. The Watch follows evidence towards the legendary Fist of the First Men and awaits **Qhorin Halfhand**.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-14"></a>
### Ch. 14: Arya IV

**POV SROMX:** [Arya Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_ARYA.md) (through Ch. 64).

**≈28 Feb 299 AC**  
*Riverlands* · *abandoned holdfast*  
**ARYA** · **AMORY LORCH** · **GENDRY** · **JAQEN**

Lannister men attack **Yoren**'s refuge. **Arya**, **Gendry** and their companions fight desperately, and Arya releases imprisoned **Jaqen H'ghar**, Rorge and Biter before escaping through a hidden tunnel; Yoren and many recruits perish.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-15"></a>
### Ch. 15: Tyrion III

**≈7 Mar 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**TYRION** · **BRONN** · **CERSEI LANNISTER** · **PYCELLE** · **LITTLEFINGER** · **PODRICK PAYNE** · **VARYS**

**Tyrion** examines Stannis's proclamation of **Joffrey**'s illegitimacy and uses **Varys** to trace the capital's hidden political manoeuvres. He balances threats from Stannis and Renly while preparing the city for possible siege.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-16"></a>
### Ch. 16: Bran II

**≈17 Mar 299 AC**  
*Winterfell*  
**BRAN** · **HODOR** · **LUWIN** · **OSHA** · **RODRIK**

**Bran** prepares to receive northern guests and hears troubling news from the Hornwood lands. The Frey wards disrupt the castle's routine; his green and wolf dreams begin to blur memories with prophecy.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-17"></a>
### Ch. 17: Tyrion IV

**≈26 Mar 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**TYRION** · **BRONN** · **CERSEI LANNISTER** · **PYCELLE** · **JOFFREY** · **LANCEL LANNISTER** · **LITTLEFINGER** · **PODRICK PAYNE** · **VARYS**

**Tyrion** tests the small councillors' secrecy by giving **Pycelle**, **Varys** and **Littlefinger** different marriage plans for **Myrcella**. He intends to identify whoever informs **Cersei** while advancing a genuine Dornish alliance.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-18"></a>

**SANSA** · **DONTOS** · **JOFFREY** · **SANDOR**

**Sansa** secretly meets **Dontos Hollard**, who offers to help her escape. Her hope for rescue remains fragile as **Joffrey** and **Sandor Clegane** remind her how dangerous the royal court has become.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-19"></a>
### Ch. 19: Arya V

*King's Landing*

**Tyrion** exposes **Pycelle** as **Cersei**'s informant and orders him arrested. He secures **Myrcella**'s proposed betrothal to Trystane Martell, extracting concessions from Cersei while concealing the danger that surrounds the princess.

At Winterfell's harvest feast, **Bran** meets **Jojen** and **Meera Reed**, whose knowledge of his strange dreams unsettles him. The Reeds identify his growing abilities, while Bran fears visions that echo his crippling fall.

**Catelyn** arrives at **Renly**'s splendid host and sees how popularity and Reach wealth support his competing claim. **Brienne of Tarth** proves her prowess; news that **Stannis** has besieged Storm's End abruptly redirects the army.

**Jon** meets **Craster** and **Gilly**, whose plight reveals the monstrous bargains underlying the wildling's household. **Jeor Mormont** seeks information about Mance Rayder and the Others before leading the Watch farther north.

*King's Landing*  
**TYRION** · **ALLISER THORNE** · **BRONN** · **CERSEI LANNISTER** · **CLEOS FREY** · **PYCELLE** · **ILYN** · **BYWATER** · **LANCEL LANNISTER** · **MERYN TRANT** · **LITTLEFINGER** · **SANSA** · **SHAGGA** · **VARYS**

## SEQ 5: THE BROKEN CROWN

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-32"></a>
### Ch. 32: Sansa III

[SROMX | Sansa Stark | Full Book](SROMX_COK_POV_SANSA.md) (complete POV through Ch. 65)

**≈13 Jun 299 AC**  
*King's Landing*  
**SANSA** · **BRONN** · **DONTOS** · **JOFFREY** · **LANCEL LANNISTER** · **MERYN TRANT** · **SANDOR** · **TYRION**

**Sansa** is publicly beaten after news of **Robb**'s western victories. **Tyrion** intervenes and **Sandor** helps her, but the brutality makes clear that her safety depends on the whims of her captors.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-33"></a>
### Ch. 33: Catelyn IV

**≈12 Jun 299 AC**  
*Storm's End* · *road to Riverrun*  
**CATELYN** · **BRIENNE OF** · **RENLY BARATHEON**

A shadow assassin kills **Renly** inside his tent before **Catelyn** and **Brienne**. Blamed for the murder, they flee the camp as Renly's coalition collapses and **Stannis** claims its armies.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-34"></a>
### Ch. 34: Jon IV

**≈5 Jul 299 AC**  
*Fist of the First Men*  
**JON** · **MORMONT** · **SAMWELL TARLY**

**Jon** reaches the ancient hilltop stronghold with **Jeor Mormont** and the Watch, discovering a cache of dragonglass blades and an old black cloak. The hidden weapons suggest someone anticipated the dangers gathering beyond the Wall.

<a id="cok-csrm-ch-35"></a>
### Ch. 35: Bran V

**≈27 Jun 299 AC**

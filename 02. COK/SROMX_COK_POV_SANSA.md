# 1. Narrative Context

Following **Eddard Stark's** execution, **Sansa** remains a hostage in **King's Landing**. Still officially betrothed to **Joffrey Baratheon**, she must denounce her own family while her brother **Robb** fights the **Lannisters**. Joffrey has already ordered his Kingsguard to beat her when the news from the war displeases him. Courtesy now conceals fear and grief rather than innocent admiration for the royal court. Cut off from **Winterfell** and her family, Sansa longs for a safe means of escape, yet possesses no reliable protector or practical plan.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At **Joffrey Baratheon's** nameday tourney in the **Red Keep**, **Sansa Stark** hides bruises inflicted by his guards. When the drunken **Dontos Hollard** faces execution for appearing at the lists unprepared, she suggests making him the king's fool instead; **Sandor Clegane** reinforces her intervention. She watches **Tommen** embarrassed by a training dummy before **Tyrion Lannister** arrives with armed retainers to govern in his father's name. He speaks kindly, but Sansa no longer trusts Lannister assurances.

A secret summons later leads her to the **godswood**, where Dontos vows to become her rescuer. Though he has no ship or immediate means of departure, Sansa agrees to meet him discreetly. On her return Sandor intercepts her, mocks her belief in true knights and warns that courtiers are liars. When news of **Robb's** victory at **Oxcross** arrives, Joffrey orders Sansa beaten. Dontos tries to divert the punishment with a mock assault, but **Boros Blount** strikes her and tears her gown. Tyrion halts the violence, and Sandor throws her his cloak. Treated in the **Tower of the Hand**, Sansa hears Tyrion discuss ending her betrothal, but refuses his proposed mountain-clan guards because confinement would obstruct Dontos's plan.

As **Stannis** advances upon **King's Landing**, Dontos again asks for patience: the river and city gates are guarded. From a roof above Maegor's Holdfast, Sansa sees a horizon of smoke and burning waterfront. Sandor confronts her about the brutality of warfare and his contempt for knights. After a nightmare of an earlier city riot, Sansa wakes to her first menstrual bleeding. Fearing immediate marriage to Joffrey, she tries to burn the bloodstained bedding. **Cersei Lannister** summons her and speaks bitterly of childbirth, royal marriages and the dangers of love.

When the **Battle of the Blackwater** begins, Sansa hears Joffrey's boasts, joins the common prayers at the castle sept and takes her appointed seat beside Cersei in the **Queen's Ballroom**. **Ilyn Payne** waits with her father's sword. As messengers describe the burning river and attacks on the gates, Cersei drinks heavily, speaks of fear as the basis of loyalty and admits that Ilyn is there to prevent either woman from being captured alive. When the defence falters, Cersei deserts the assembly to recover Joffrey. Sansa instead calms the women, enlists **Moon Boy** to distract them and helps the injured **Lancel Lannister** obtain treatment.

In her dark bedchamber Sansa finds Sandor drunk, bloodied and preparing to desert after the wildfire. He presses a dagger to her throat and demands a song. She sings a hymn asking the Mother for mercy and feels moisture beneath the blood when she touches his face. Sandor leaves his torn white cloak and departs. At dawn the bells announce the city's rescue; Dontos brings confused but jubilant reports of **Tywin Lannister** and Tyrell forces defeating Stannis.

At the ensuing victory court, Sansa watches Tywin take the Hand's chain and the **Tyrells** receive royal honours. **Garlan Tyrell** proposes his sister **Margaery** for Joffrey; Cersei and the **High Septon** support dissolution of Sansa's betrothal, and the king agrees. Sansa conceals her delight but is ordered to remain at court as a ward. She sees **Petyr Baelish** rewarded with **Harrenhal** and authority over the Trident as captives face judgment. That night Dontos warns that her captivity persists, proposes escape during the coming royal wedding, and gives her a delicate silver hairnet set with dark purple gems.

The record ends at her closing scene of **COK Ch. 65**, rather than at COK's final source unit.

The Blackwater chapters encompass night and the following dawn; they do not form discrete calendar instants. No authorised ASOIAF reader-standardised elapsed-time anchor has been established.

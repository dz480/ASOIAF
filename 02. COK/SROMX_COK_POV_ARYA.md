# 1. Narrative Context

At the close of *A Game of Thrones*, **Joffrey Baratheon** has ordered **Eddard Stark** executed in **King's Landing**. His daughter **Arya Stark** has lost her father's protection, escaped the seizure of the Stark household and failed to reach the ship meant to carry her home. **Yoren**, a recruiter for the **Night's Watch**, prevents her from rushing to the execution and cuts her hair to disguise her as an orphan boy. Her sword **Needle**, given to her by **Jon Snow**, survives the flight. As the rival royal claims and fighting around the **Riverlands** expand, Yoren prepares to take Arya north with a company of recruits; she hopes to reach **Winterfell** without revealing her identity.

# 4. Narrative Overview

**Arya Stark** leaves **King's Landing** hidden among **Yoren**'s northbound recruits, travelling as the boy Arry. **Hot Pie** and **Lommy Greenhands** torment her over her hair, size and sword, provoking a fight that Yoren breaks up; **Gendry**, called the Bull for his horned helmet, is the first boy to warn her of an attack. With **Needle** concealed and the executions in the capital still vivid, Arya clings to the prospect of **Winterfell**, or perhaps seeing **Jon** at the Wall. The column includes dangerous prisoners in a chained wagon: **Jaqen H'ghar**, **Rorge** and **Biter**.

Refugees stream south while Yoren presses north. At an inn, Arya hears contradictory stories of wolves, armies and **Robb Stark**; the company also buries the sick **Praed**. **Gold cloaks** arrive with a warrant for Gendry. Yoren refuses to surrender him, turns his sword against the officer and rides on expecting pursuit. Avoiding the kingsroad, the party moves through burned settlements and abandoned farmland near the **Gods Eye**. At a ruined holdfast they rescue an infant girl later called **Weasel** and a severely injured woman who subsequently dies. Hunger, wolves and the approach of armed forces steadily narrow their choices.

Yoren fortifies an abandoned holdfast as **Amory Lorch**'s soldiers approach. A violent night assault overwhelms the makeshift defence. Arya searches the burning stables and tries to save the youngest children before noticing the prison wagon and releasing Jaqen, Rorge and Biter with an axe. She escapes through a tunnel as the defenders are killed or dispersed. Yoren never rejoins them. The remaining youngsters flee across woodland, trying to avoid soldiers and find food; wounded Lommy is increasingly unable to walk. Gendry recognises that Arya is a girl, and she eventually tells him she is **Eddard Stark**'s daughter. While investigating a lakeside village, Gendry is captured; Arya and Hot Pie attempt a rescue but are seized by **Gregor Clegane**'s men. The captors reach Lommy and kill him when he cannot march. The small girl escapes into the woods; her ultimate fate is not established.

The prisoners are kept in a storehouse near the Gods Eye where **the Tickler**, under Gregor, extracts information through torture. Arya sees people selected, maimed and murdered, memorising her tormentors while concealing her fear and parentage. **Polliver** takes Needle; **Dunsen** takes Gendry's horned helmet. Marched to **Harrenhal**, the survivors are stripped, washed and divided into servants. Arya gives her name as Weasel and enters the **Wailing Tower** under the abusive understeward **Weese**. Hot Pie works in the kitchens and Gendry in the forge. The vast, partly ruined castle holds **Tywin Lannister**'s army, northern captives and **Vargo Hoat**'s mercenaries. Arya learns that invisibility as a lowly servant can bring overheard information, but it offers little safety.

Jaqen, now free and serving at Harrenhal, tells Arya that 3 deaths are owed for the 3 men she rescued from the fire. Hearing **Chiswyck** tell a brutal story about an assault on a young woman, Arya whispers his name. He soon falls to his death, leaving Arya convinced that Jaqen's power is real. Her second chosen victim is Weese, whose mistreatment she can scarcely escape. As Tywin marches out, she realises too late that she has squandered a chance to name a greater Lannister target. Weese's own spotted dog tears out his throat. Jaqen's gesture confirms his fulfilment of the second death.

When **Robett Glover** and other northmen reach Harrenhal as prisoners of **Vargo Hoat**'s company, Arya attempts to persuade Gendry to help free them; he refuses the risk. She instead confronts Jaqen in the godswood and uses the threat of naming him as her third death to enlist his assistance. With Rorge and Biter, he turns a pretence of delivering boiling broth into an assault on the dungeon guards, releasing the northern captives. In the fighting the castle passes from **Amory Lorch** to **Roose Bolton**, supported by the **Brave Companions**; Lorch is thrown to a bear. Jaqen changes his face before Arya, gives her an iron coin and the words *valar morghulis*, and departs. Bolton selects Arya, calling herself Nan, as his cupbearer.

Serving Bolton brings Arya close enough to hear **Frey** officers discussing the war and **Robb**'s changed marriage plans; she also hears reports that **Bran** and **Rickon** have died, claims she is powerless to verify. **Qyburn** tends Bolton, while **Elmar Frey**, a young squire, unknowingly reveals that the Stark king has broken the marriage intended for him. Bolton makes clear that when he departs he will leave Harrenhal to Hoat and Arya there as a servant. She acts that night, taking a map and dagger from Bolton's chambers and persuading Gendry and Hot Pie to accompany her. Masquerading as a messenger of Bolton, she acquires horses and approaches a guarded postern. She distracts the guard with Jaqen's iron coin, kills him and unbars the door. The 3 ride away into the rain, free of Harrenhal but still without a secure route home.

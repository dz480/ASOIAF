# 1. Narrative Context

After **Robert Baratheon's** death and **Eddard Stark's** execution, the boy king **Joffrey** holds **King's Landing** for **House Lannister**, while **Robb Stark**, **Stannis Baratheon** and **Renly Baratheon** challenge the new political order. **Tywin Lannister**, fighting in the riverlands, has authorised his son **Tyrion** to serve as acting Hand and restrain the reckless decisions of **Cersei** and her advisers. Tyrion approaches a capital strained by refugees, food shortages and suspicious courtiers. His authority rests on a letter from his father, the guards and companions he has brought with him, and the uncertain obedience of the queen's council. Although Tywin forbade it, Tyrion has also brought **Shae** close to the city, creating a private vulnerability amid his public political ambitions.

# 4. Narrative Overview

Arriving at **King's Landing** after **Eddard Stark's** execution, **Tyrion Lannister** produces **Tywin's** appointment letter and forces his way past **Ser Mandon Moore** into the **Small Council**. His sister **Cersei** resents his arrival, but he accepts the acting Handship and studies the rival interests of **Varys**, **Petyr Baelish** and **Grand Maester Pycelle**. The city's refugees and ruined harvest make siege a looming danger. Defying Tywin's orders, Tyrion keeps **Shae** close to the capital under a discreet arrangement, although he knows that affection can be used against him.

He first moves against the existing royal machinery. At an intimate dinner, Tyrion confronts **Janos Slynt** with the Gold Cloaks' part in killing Robert's bastard children and with their betrayal of Ned. Slynt is detained and sent to **the Wall**, while **Jacelyn Bywater** receives command of the **City Watch**. Tyrion hires and trusts **Bronn's** ruthless soldiers while acknowledging the risk that his bodyguards serve for payment rather than fidelity. News of **Stannis Baratheon's** letters accusing Joffrey of illegitimate birth divides the council and spreads fears of further rebellion.

Tyrion cultivates uncertainty among the councillors by presenting different proposed marriages for **Myrcella Baratheon** to Pycelle, Littlefinger and Varys. Cersei's angry disclosure identifies Pycelle as her informant; Tyrion's real purpose is to secure the Dornish match with **Trystane Martell**, supported by concessions to **Doran Martell**. **Cleos Frey** brings proposals concerning the captive **Jaime**, and Tyrion tests whether exchanges and marriage diplomacy can relieve pressure while the Lannisters fight on several fronts. The city's **Alchemists' Guild**, represented by **Hallyne**, becomes central to Tyrion's stockpiling of wildfire. He humiliates and arrests Pycelle after discovering his long service to Cersei's interests and his account of **Jon Arryn's** death.

When **Alliser Thorne** arrives from **the Night's Watch** with warnings from the north, he finds that the court is distracted and unprepared to take the threat seriously. Tyrion's conflict with Cersei grows more intimate when her young companion **Lancel Lannister** attempts to secure Pycelle's release. Tyrion threatens to expose Lancel's sexual relationship with the queen and converts him into an informer. He also visits Shae by way of **Chataya's** establishment and **Alayaya**, all while fearing that his sister will identify the woman he truly loves.

After reports of **Renly Baratheon's** murder, the military picture changes. Stannis stands to gain men who once served his brother, so Tyrion sends Littlefinger to negotiate with the **Tyrells**, whose forces could transform the balance of power. Tyrion meanwhile sends Myrcella from the harbour to Dorne, separating her from an increasingly perilous court. On the royal party's way back to the **Red Keep**, a thrown object provokes **Joffrey**, and the hungry crowds riot. **Sansa Stark** is endangered, noblemen and guards are attacked, and **the High Septon** is killed. Tyrion survives the violence but recognises how deeply the people hate the king and his advisers.

With **Stannis** advancing, Tyrion learns from Lancel that Cersei plans to conceal **Tommen** at **Rosby** and quietly takes measures to secure the younger prince himself. He relies on **Varys** to arrange Shae's placement in **Lollys Stokeworth's** household, sets the mountain clans to harass the approaching army and fortifies the **Blackwater Rush** with hulks, wildfire and a great chain. **Shagga**, **Timett** and the other clansmen provide dangerous irregular assistance. When Varys uncovers the wealthy **Antler Men**, Tyrion instructs Bywater to suppress their plan to open the **Old Gate** during the battle.

Cersei then confronts Tyrion with an imprisoned **Alayaya**, whom she wrongly believes to be his lover. When she threatens abuse of the young woman, Tyrion threatens corresponding harm to Tommen; inwardly, he is appalled by the line he has crossed, and fears for Shae's safety. He continues organising the city's defences despite personal exhaustion and distrust. When Stannis's fleet enters the river, the wildfire traps its ships in terrifying green fire. Yet enemy men reach the banks and batter the gates. Joffrey is withdrawn, **Sandor Clegane** refuses to lead another sortie into the flames, and Tyrion personally rallies the frightened defenders to charge from the **Mud Gate**.

Outside the walls, Tyrion and **Ser Mandon Moore** attack the besiegers, then scramble across shattered rivercraft as the battle becomes confused. Moore extends a gauntleted hand to help Tyrion across a gap, only to slash him savagely across the face. **Podrick Payne** intervenes and the knight falls into the river. Tyrion loses consciousness, badly wounded.

His final COK chapter follows a fevered recovery and disturbing dreams rather than the victory celebrations. Tyrion discovers that his father has returned to become Hand, that the Tower of the Hand and his men have passed out of his control, and that several allies were killed or displaced. Podrick remains at his side; Tyrion demands a different physician and asks after Bronn and the suspected attempt on his life. The extensive wound to his face and the political dispossession are real, but he cannot prove who sent Mandon. His Handship ends without certainty about the attack or the fate of his private relationship with Shae.

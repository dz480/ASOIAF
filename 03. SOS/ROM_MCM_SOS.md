## 1. Master Scope & Source Profile

- **Primary narrative:** *A Storm of Swords* (George R. R. Martin, 2000), supplied unabridged EPUB (Random House, 2003; ISBN 978-0-553-89787-6).
- **Eligible source units:** SOS Prologue; SOS Ch. 1–80; SOS Epilogue. The supplied RTE establishes the same 82-unit sequence.
- **Frozen continuity source:** [Master Character Registry (MCR)](../SERIES%20CONTINUITY/MASTER_CHARACTER_REGISTRY.csv), currently at **COK Ch. 69**, contains 376 established historical identities. Existing series IDs, first-series appearances and verified cumulative counts are inherited where matchable.
- **Approved external reference:** [Leo King, *A Spreadsheet of Ice and Fire*](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1K8DJTFUUIZvQsgnkxv3d-142SXTcmnTV4YuHsNubHjA/edit), which records **420 explicitly named SOS characters** under its broader named-character counting method.
- **Additional chapter evidence:** Reader-supplied `ASOIAF (1).xlsx`, worksheet `SoS`, is a per-chapter participant matrix. The **285 qualifying named rows** with enactment markers produce this map's provisional source-indexed roster and chapter data. The separately supplied `Deaths` worksheet supports reported book-end fates.
- **Interpretive difference:** Leo King's 420 includes named characters under an explicit-name method; the 285-row source matrix maps named enacted participation. Neither 420 nor 285 is a universal objective census of all possible character-mentions and minor actors.
- **Direct-presence rule:** Speaking, nonspeaking and genuinely functional nonordinary participation qualify. A named offstage report alone does not create a Chapter Appearance. A repeated appearance within one chapter counts once.
- **Book-end vs live continuity:** All SOS deaths, disappearances, promotions and book-end counts in this file are projected advance data. The existing MCR remains unchanged at COK-end.
- **Quality statement:** Whole-book chapter matrix coverage, COK-end baseline inheritance and complete current-book roster are implemented. An independent line-by-line EPUB audit of all upper-tier appearances and all early-series historical mismatches is not certified. See Section 6.

### Verification and limitations
1. **Cast reconciliation:** 285 enacted named source rows; 146 matched directly to pre-existing MCR identities through documented name/alias normalisation; 139 remain provisionally new to the registry at the SOS source boundary. These are MCM reference records only; the live MCR is untouched.
2. **Significance distribution:** 13 Principal; 61 Supporting; 211 Tertiary. Significance is structural; mere frequency is not a promotion rule.
3. **Chapter grid:** 927 source-marked character–chapter associations across 82 narrative units. Characters with multiple scenes in the same chapter are counted once.
4. **Nonordinary participation:** The original workbook's numeric count cells omit some marked later appearances by reanimated wights. In SOS Ch. 46 (Samwell III), Chett, Hake, Lark and Small Paul are active wights; those enacted encounters are included in the current-book chapter lists. Biological death and functional agency remain separate concepts.
5. **COK historical discrepancies:** 0 direct MCR versus COK MCM numeric disagreements are detected when both texts provide a parseable number. The authoritative frozen MCR value is preserved here; any inconsistency requires a separate reader-authorised historical reconciliation.
6. **Inherited unverified history:** 75 returning character(s) have an MCR baseline that is not a verified number. Their SOS count is shown, but the cumulative total remains explicitly Not verified.
7. **Upper-tier word-evidence diagnostic:** 11 Principal/Supporting rows include chapters lacking the individual's primary given-name token under a conservative lexical check. This can reflect aliases, titles, pronouns or genuinely unsupported matrix marks. Lexical checks are discovery cues, not a substitute for direct-presence adjudication.
8. **External reference limitation:** Leo King's approved spreadsheet provides a named-character census and series-name context. Its SOS total of 420 is not substituted for the enacted-roster total, and its name-frequency figures are not misrepresented as directly enacted chapter appearances.
9. **Source boundary:** SOS book events only. No character fate or promotion is imported from Feast, Dance, later novels or television adaptations.
10. **Quality certification:** **COMPLETE PROVISIONAL REFERENCE / INDEPENDENT PRIORITY AUDIT OUTSTANDING.** The six prescribed sections are assembled and the character map is usable as a structured foresight index, but a fully priority-verified status is withheld until unresolved direct-presence and historical count conflicts are cleared.

## 1. Master Scope & Source Profile

- **Primary narrative:** *A Storm of Swords* (George R. R. Martin, 2000), supplied unabridged EPUB (Random House, 2003; ISBN 978-0-553-89787-6).
- **Eligible source units:** SOS Prologue; SOS Ch. 1–80; SOS Epilogue. The supplied RTE establishes the same 82-unit sequence.
- **Frozen continuity source:** [Master Character Registry (MCR)](../SERIES%20CONTINUITY/MASTER_CHARACTER_REGISTRY.csv), currently at **COK Ch. 69**, contains 376 established historical identities. Existing series IDs, first-series appearances and verified cumulative counts are inherited where matchable.
- **Approved external reference:** [Leo King, *A Spreadsheet of Ice and Fire*](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1K8DJTFUUIZvQsgnkxv3d-142SXTcmnTV4YuHsNubHjA/edit), which records **420 explicitly named SOS characters** under its broader named-character counting method.
- **Additional chapter evidence:** Reader-supplied `ASOIAF (1).xlsx`, worksheet `SoS`, is a per-chapter participant matrix. The **285 source candidate rows, of which 281 pass the named-individual roster threshold** with enactment markers produce this map's source-indexed candidate data, subject to the ROM inclusion rule. The separately supplied `Deaths` worksheet supports reported book-end fates.
- **Interpretive difference:** Leo King's 420 includes named characters under an explicit-name method; the 285-row source candidate matrix maps candidate participation; four unnamed or title-only figures do not qualify for dedicated MCM entries. Neither 420 nor 285 is a universal objective census of all possible character-mentions and minor actors.
- **Direct-presence rule:** Speaking, nonspeaking and genuinely functional nonordinary participation qualify. A named offstage report alone does not create a Chapter Appearance. A repeated appearance within one chapter counts once.
- **Book-end vs live continuity:** All SOS deaths, disappearances, promotions and book-end counts in this file are projected advance data. The existing MCR remains unchanged at COK-end.
- **Quality statement:** Whole-book chapter matrix coverage, COK-end baseline inheritance and complete current-book roster are implemented. An independent line-by-line EPUB audit of all upper-tier appearances and all early-series historical mismatches is not certified. See Section 6.

# 1. Narrative Context

After years of rule from **Winterfell**, **Eddard Stark** welcomes **King Robert Baratheon** and his court to the **North**. Ned's acknowledged bastard **Jon Snow** has grown up alongside the Stark children but occupies an insecure place in the household, particularly under **Catelyn Stark**, who resents his presence. Jon has never known his mother. His uncle **Benjen Stark** serves in the **Night's Watch**, a sworn brotherhood guarding the immense **Wall** at the northern edge of the realm. The order once carried great prestige, although it now accepts criminals and other men with few alternatives. The king's visit forces Jon to confront his exclusion from the family's public honours just as Ned prepares for new responsibilities far from home.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At **WINTERFELL**'s royal feast, **Jon Snow** drinks with the squires while his trueborn siblings sit at the high table. He asks his uncle **Benjen Stark** to take him to the **Wall**, insisting a bastard can earn honour there. Benjen warns him about the Watch's lifelong sacrifices. Upset, Jon leaves the hall and meets **Tyrion Lannister**, who counsels him to turn his unwanted identity into a source of strength. Before departing, Jon visits the unconscious **Bran**, enduring **Catelyn Stark**'s hostility, embraces **Robb** and presents **Arya** with a slender sword they name Needle.

At **CASTLE BLACK**, Jon discovers a neglected brotherhood whose recruits lack his years of noble instruction. He repeatedly defeats **Grenn** and others, provoking both their resentment and the scorn of **Alliser Thorne**. After the recruits attack him, the one-armed smith **Donal Noye** explains that Jon has been behaving like a bully by treating unequal contests as proof of superiority. Jon begins teaching his companions instead of humiliating them. His friendship with Tyrion continues briefly, and a letter carried to **Jeor Mormont** assures him that Bran will survive. Meanwhile, Benjen has ridden into the **Haunted Forest** and failed to return.

When **Samwell Tarly** arrives, Thorne makes him a target for violent training. Jon intervenes, supported by **Pyp** and Grenn, and later hears Sam's account of being sent to the Wall by his father. Jon persuades the other boys to spare him and confronts **Rast** when he refuses. Sam becomes part of Jon's chosen brotherhood.

Jon is among the recruits selected for advancement, but Sam is not. He approaches the blind **Maester Aemon** and argues that Sam's learning will make him useful as an assistant. Aemon agrees to consider the request. At the assignment ceremony, Sam receives that position, while Jon is named **Mormont**'s personal steward. Jon initially sees humiliation in the appointment until Sam points out that it places him beside the Lord Commander himself. The 2 friends travel into the forest to take their vows before a heart tree; on the return, **Ghost** brings Jon a human hand.

Searchers find the corpses of the missing rangers **Othor** and **Jafer Flowers**. **Jaremy Rykker**, **Dywen** and Sam notice disturbing signs: the bodies show no ordinary decay, and animals recoil from them. Mormont orders the dead returned for examination. At Castle Black, Jon hears that **Robert Baratheon** has died and his father has been imprisoned. He threatens Thorne after a taunt and is confined to his cell. During the night Ghost alerts him to danger; Jon reaches the Lord Commander's quarters as the reanimated Othor attacks. After his blade fails to stop the dead man, Jon burns him with a lamp and flaming cloth, saving Mormont but badly injuring his own hand.

Recovering from his burns, Jon receives the ancestral Valyrian steel sword **Longclaw** from Mormont, now refitted with a wolf-head pommel. He also learns of **Robb**'s march south. In a conversation among the ravens, Maester Aemon speaks of the conflict between duty and love, then reveals that he was born **Aemon Targaryen**. He leaves Jon to decide what his oath requires.

News of **Ned**'s death brings Jon to the limit of that commitment. He secretly saddles a horse and rides south to join Robb, but Ghost and a party of friends led by Pyp and Grenn intercept him near **Mole's Town**. They recite the Watch's vows, and Jon returns. Mormont, who expected him to attempt departure, tells him that the approaching danger beyond the Wall demands his service. Jon accepts that the Watch is his place and pledges to remain for the great ranging.

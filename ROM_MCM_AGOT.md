# ROM MASTER CHARACTER MAP: A GAME OF THRONES (AGOT)

**Protocol:** ROM MCM · **Scope:** Whole Book · **Source identifier:** **GOT** · **Status:** Provisional — Priority Issues · **Compiled:** 12 Oct 2026

> **Whole-book foresight reference:** Contains spoilers for the complete first novel. It is not a reader-progress update and does not initialise or advance the live MCR.

## 1. Master Scope & Source Profile

- **Work:** *A Game of Thrones*, George R. R. Martin (1996), Book 1 of *A Song of Ice and Fire*.
- **Boundary:** Complete **GOT** through **GOT Ch. 72 (Daenerys X)**; Prologue and 72 numbered chapters, 73 eligible narrative units.
- **Primary authority:** Project-supplied full EPUB, excluding maps, preliminaries, appendices and supplemental matter.
- **Reader navigation:** Project-supplied RTE chapter order, 1–72; three-letter protocol acronym **GOT**. AGOT is the conventional series shorthand.
- **ROM authority:** `ROM_MASTER_CHARACTER_MAP.md` v13.7; `ROM_CHARACTER_MAP.md` v10.45; `ROM_TERMINOLOGY_STANDARD.md` v10.73, `dz480/CG-Protocols`, retrieved 12 Oct 2026.
- **Supplementary chapter evidence:** A Wiki of Ice and Fire, AGOT chapter Appearing/Mentioned listings; cross-checked against the EPUB for the explicitly verified appearance registers only.
- **Academic dataset:** Connaughton et al., [ASOIAF-data-and_codes](https://github.com/colm-connaughton/ASOIAF-data-and_codes), inspected for structure. Its binary AGOT workbook was not successfully imported and its unverified network-derived appearances were **not** used as counts.
- **Evidence policy:** A name-search hit is merely a candidate, not a Chapter Appearance. Only directly enacted presence contributes to confirmed distinct-chapter counts. Offstage and remembered people can appear in the roster with 0 direct appearances.
- **Data-quality designation:** **Provisional — Priority Issues**, because some Principal/Supporting Chapter Appearance lists and latest direct-presence coordinates remain unaudited; their counts are explicitly **Not verified**, not guessed. Remaining Tertiary counts are deliberately exempt.

## 2. Full Character Roster

**Reference convention:** Each appearance cell renders First ⇤ / Latest ⇥ / cumulative distinct-chapter appearances. All unresolved values are identified, rather than converting name mentions into physical appearances. Names and role identities are constrained to book-one knowledge.

## 6. Verification & Source Notes

### Validated scope and identity controls

- **Chapter structure:** 73 narrative units matched against the supplied EPUB and RTE schedule. Prologue counts as 1 eligible unit.
- **Direct presence:** A physically enacted character is counted at most once per chapter; mentions, reports, recollections, dream-representations and historical names do not themselves count.
- **No later-novel import:** Three newborn dragons are described only by their AGOT-established characteristics rather than importing their later-established proper names into this book-one roster.
- **Named animals:** Only select independently acting direwolves with sustained narrative agency are individually rostered. Routine named companions are discussed through their associated human characters.
- **Status evidence:** Confirmed-deceased census members are marked †; uncertain fates are excluded from the confirmed-death count.
- **MCR firewall:** Under current `ROM_MASTER_CHARACTER_REGISTRY.md` v1.3, initial live series registration occurs after completion of Book 1; this foresight MCM does not trigger that event.

### Priority-tier unresolved fields

- **42 Principal/Supporting records** require a complete direct-presence audit for current-book chapter totals and Book End cumulative sums. Those fields are marked Not verified / Not calculated, never shown as a guessed count.

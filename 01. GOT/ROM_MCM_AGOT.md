## 1. Master Scope & Source Profile

- **Work:** *A Game of Thrones*, George R. R. Martin (1996), Book 1 of *A Song of Ice and Fire*.
- **Boundary:** Complete **GOT** through **GOT Ch. 72 (Daenerys X)**; Prologue and 72 numbered chapters, 73 eligible narrative units.
- **Primary authority:** Project-supplied full EPUB, excluding maps, preliminaries, appendices and supplemental matter.
- **Reader navigation:** Project-supplied RTE chapter order, 1–72; three-letter protocol acronym **GOT**. AGOT is the conventional series shorthand.
- **ROM authority:** `ROM_MASTER_CHARACTER_MAP.md` v13.7; `ROM_CHARACTER_MAP.md` v10.45; `ROM_TERMINOLOGY_STANDARD.md` v10.73, `dz480/CG-Protocols`, retrieved 12 Oct 2026.
- **Supplementary chapter evidence:** A Wiki of Ice and Fire, all 73 AGOT chapter Appearing/Mentioned lists (Prologue and chapters 1–72), cross-checked against the primary EPUB for all 55 Principal/Supporting records. Direct-presence counts use the AGOT narrative layer rather than mentions or remembered/dreamed episodes.
- **Academic dataset:** Connaughton et al., [ASOIAF-data-and_codes](https://github.com/colm-connaughton/ASOIAF-data-and_codes), inspected for structure. Its binary AGOT workbook was not successfully imported and its unverified network-derived appearances were **not** used as counts.
- **Evidence policy:** A name-search hit is merely a candidate, not a Chapter Appearance. Only directly enacted presence contributes to confirmed distinct-chapter counts. Offstage and remembered people can appear in the roster with 0 direct appearances.
- **Data-quality designation:** **Satisfactory for Use — Priority Verified.** All 55 Principal/Supporting records now have chapter-by-chapter direct-presence lists, source-checked first/latest coordinates, and verified Book 1 counts with a cumulative baseline of 0. Remaining Tertiary totals are intentionally exempt, and their unresolved first/latest coordinates are explicitly identified.

## 6. Verification & Source Notes

**14 Oct 2026 — historical appearance-source reconciliation:** Compared all 55 GOT Principal/Supporting lists against the reader-supplied published [ASOIAF comprehensive character appearance workbook](https://www.reddit.com/r/pureasoiaf/comments/e0s67x), using its **GoT** tab marker grid (73 narrative units). **52** of the priority records matched the author's indexed identity; **14** chapter lists differed and have been reconciled, including first/latest chapter and book-end counts where affected. The other three priority records (Jon Arryn, Rhaego, Stannis Baratheon) have no qualifying direct GoT appearance according to their established MCM treatment and remain at zero. Distinct direct-presence chapter markers were imported; recollections, corpses and other source-definition disagreements are recorded as source-based adjustments, not newly invented enactments. This source-based workbook reconciliation supersedes the older self-described chapter lists, but does not independently establish textual certification across all scenes. The equivalent historical MCR data were reconciled without advancing its GOT-end reading boundary.



### Validated scope and identity controls

- **Chapter structure:** 73 narrative units matched against the supplied EPUB and RTE schedule. Prologue counts as 1 eligible unit.
- **Direct presence:** A physically enacted character is counted at most once per chapter; mentions, reports, recollections, dream-representations and historical names do not themselves count.
- **No later-novel import:** Three newborn dragons are described only by their AGOT-established characteristics rather than importing their later-established proper names into this book-one roster.
- **Named animals:** Only select independently acting direwolves with sustained narrative agency are individually rostered. Routine named companions are discussed through their associated human characters.
- **Status evidence:** Confirmed-deceased census members are marked †; uncertain fates are excluded from the confirmed-death count.

- **Priority register coverage:** All **55 Principal/Supporting records** (14 Principal and 41 Supporting) have source-checked first/latest direct-presence coordinates, Book Chapter Counts, Book End cumulative counts and ascending chapter lists.

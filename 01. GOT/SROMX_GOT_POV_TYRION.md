# 1. Narrative Context

In **Westeros**, **King Robert Baratheon** has travelled to **Winterfell** after the death of his Hand, **Jon Arryn**, bringing the royal household into the domain of **House Stark**. **Tyrion Lannister**, the younger brother of **Cersei** and **Jaime Lannister**, accompanies the king's party. Though wealthy, educated and politically connected, Tyrion occupies a difficult place within his powerful family: his father **Tywin** disdains him, and his disability exposes him to contempt from strangers and relatives alike. **Bran Stark**, recently injured in a fall, lies unconscious while tensions simmer beneath the royal visit. Tyrion spends much of his time reading, drinking and observing the people around him, with plans to travel north and see the **Wall**.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At **WINTERFELL**, **Tyrion Lannister** leaves his books to find **Joffrey** refusing to express sympathy for the injured **Bran Stark**. Tyrion strikes the prince and rebukes him, then troubles **Jaime** and **Cersei** by suggesting that Bran may recover and speak. He travels north with **Benjen Stark**, **Jon Snow** and **Yoren**, discussing dragons, the experience of bastardry and the true conditions of the **Night's Watch**.

At **CASTLE BLACK**, **Jeor Mormont** explains the brotherhood's dwindling numbers, neglected fortifications and missing rangers, including Benjen. Tyrion provokes **Alliser Thorne**, ascends the **Wall** and bids farewell to Jon and **Ghost** above the vast northern wilderness.

Returning south, Tyrion is arrested by **Catelyn Stark**, who accuses him of supplying the dagger used against Bran. On the **HIGH ROAD** he denies the allegation and challenges the account supplied by **Littlefinger**. When mountain raiders attack, Catelyn arms him; Tyrion fights beside **Bronn**, **Rodrik Cassel** and the escort, which suffers heavy losses.

At **THE EYRIE**, **Lysa Arryn** confines Tyrion in a sky cell exposed to the open air. He persuades the gaoler **Mord** to take a message to the court, demands trial by combat and finds Bronn willing to act as his champion. Bronn's victory occurs between Tyrion's selected chapters. Freed, Tyrion travels through the **Mountains of the Moon**, recounts his painful first marriage to **Tysha**, and negotiates with mountain clansmen led by **Gunthor** and **Shagga**, offering arms and the Vale's wealth.

Tyrion reaches **Tywin Lannister**'s host at the **Inn at the Crossroads**. Tywin outlines the war against Stark and Tully armies, but the clans insist Tyrion fight beside them until his promises are honoured. Bronn introduces **Shae**, who becomes Tyrion's companion. At the **Green Fork**, Tyrion rides in **Gregor Clegane**'s vulnerable vanguard, fights desperately and survives a clash with a mounted knight. **Ulf** and **Conn** are among the many fallen clansmen. Tywin admits that he expected the flank to collapse and entice the northern army into a trap, but their enemy was **Roose Bolton** while **Robb Stark** marched west.

News then arrives of Jaime's capture and the relief of **Riverrun**. With Joffrey threatening to destabilise the capital and Robert's brothers preparing rival claims, Tywin appoints Tyrion acting Hand to govern **KING'S LANDING**. Tywin forbids him to bring Shae; Tyrion returns to her and announces his intention to do so anyway.

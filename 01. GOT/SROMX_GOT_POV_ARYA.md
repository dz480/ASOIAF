# 1. Narrative Context

**Winterfell**, the ancestral seat of **House Stark**, hosts **King Robert Baratheon** and his royal household while **Eddard Stark** prepares to serve as the king's Hand. Ned's younger daughter **Arya Stark** is expected to practise embroidery, courtesy and the other accomplishments taught by **Septa Mordane**, but she prefers riding, fighting and the company of her half-brother **Jon Snow**. Her elder sister **Sansa Stark** excels at the prescribed accomplishments and is promised to **Prince Joffrey**. Amid the royal visit, Arya must navigate comparisons with Sansa, her mother's expectations and the social rules governing a northern noble girl. Her young direwolf **Nymeria** remains her closest companion.

# 4. Narrative Overview

At **WINTERFELL**, **Arya Stark** struggles through embroidery lessons beside her accomplished sister **Sansa** and **Princess Myrcella**. She runs out after **Septa Mordane** reprimands her, collects **Nymeria** and joins **Jon Snow** to watch the boys training. Below them **Joffrey** provokes **Robb Stark** by demanding live steel, while **Sandor Clegane** challenges **Rodrik Cassel**'s authority. Jon sympathises with Arya's frustration, but she returns to find her mother waiting.

In **KING'S LANDING**, a fraught supper sends Arya to her chamber, where **Eddard Stark** discovers **Needle**, Jon's farewell gift. She grieves for **Mycah** and **Lady**, whose deaths followed the quarrel on the southward road. Ned lets her retain the weapon, warns that his enemies threaten the family and engages **Syrio Forel** to instruct her in Braavosi water dancing.

Syrio later makes her chase cats through the **Red Keep**. Following a one-eared tom, Arya becomes lost among enormous dragon skulls and passes into hidden tunnels. She overhears 2 disguised men, **Varys** and **Illyrio Mopatis**, discussing a looming war and the danger of events proceeding too quickly. Emerging outside the castle, she struggles to re-enter and tell Ned what she has heard, but cannot identify the speakers or explain their conversation convincingly. **Yoren**, visiting with news from the roads, interrupts.

Later, **Ser Meryn Trant** arrives with 5 armed Lannister men to seize her. Syrio refuses to surrender his pupil and incapacitates the guards with a wooden practice sword, ordering Arya to flee. She does not see how his confrontation with Trant ends. Discovering dead Stark retainers at the stables, she tries to saddle a horse; an unnamed stableboy attempts to capture her for the queen's reward, and Arya kills him with Needle. With the castle gates guarded, she escapes through secret underground passages.

Alone in the city, Arya catches pigeons for food and searches for the **Wind Witch**, the ship arranged to take her home, but finds soldiers at the docks. Summoning bells draw her to the **Great Sept of Baelor**, where she climbs a statue and sees her injured father confess publicly to treason. Joffrey unexpectedly orders **Ilyn Payne** to execute him. Arya rushes forward, but Yoren seizes her, orders her to look away and drags her from the crowd. He recovers Needle and cuts at her hair to disguise her as a boy.
